# Catalog Glossary

This skill documents domain concepts, business definitions, and data models used across the catalog systems.

## Key Definitions
- **Item / SKU**: The discrete sellable unit.
- **BOM (Bill of Materials)**: Hierarchical decomposition of parts and subassemblies.
- **Revision**: Versioned definition of an engineering or catalog item.
